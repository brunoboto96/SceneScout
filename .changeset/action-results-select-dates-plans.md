`scout_select` matches the value against the dropdown's options before picking: an exact value or label, either ignoring case, then a label it starts with ("Low" picks "Low — minor impact"). A value that matches no option, or several, is refused at once with the options listed, instead of waiting out the action limit. Typing into a date or time field puts an obvious near miss in the field's format (a plain date into a date-and-time field is entered at T00:00) and refuses anything else naming the format the field takes, instead of failing with "Malformed value". A click forced past something on top of its target names that element, and says when it came after a write-policy block. `scout_run_plan` takes `onViolation: "continue"` for a sweep of independent steps: a new error status is listed on its step's line and the plan goes on, while a failed step, a policy refusal or any other violation still stops it.
