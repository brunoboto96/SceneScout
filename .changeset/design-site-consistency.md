Design audit: pages are compared with each other. Each audit keeps the page's button heights, radii, h1 treatment, tab styles, grays, font sizes and paddings with its score. From four audited pages, the audit result ends with a SITE CONSISTENCY block and the report adds "Design consistency across pages", listing each property's values, the dominant one and the pages that alone use another; a page loses 2 points for each button height, radius, header, tab style or gray no other page uses (at most 10, never for a value its own rule already charged). The report's score table ranks by that score, keeps the page-alone score in brackets, and gains a Why column with the two largest deductions. `scenescout check` is unchanged.
