Snapshots list the page's frames (same- or cross-origin, title, size, hidden ones as a count) and say their contents were not explored, and a page whose content is all in frames is no longer called a dead end. A write that a cross-origin frame, such as an embedded third-party form, sends outside the app is refused in every mode except destructive, including a form it aims at the top window and a popup it opens on its own site. On the app's sign-in pages a captcha frame's writes still go out, outside observe.
