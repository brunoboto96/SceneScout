Cost and watched tickets. `scout_ticket_status` lists the tickets a project filed (with `scout_ticket_file` or `scenescout export`) and whether each is still open in GitHub or Jira and its problem still open in the project, reading each tracker at most once every 5 minutes and only one the server's settings point at. In the optional Claude Code mod, the guided panel says before **Start** what a check of that thoroughness uses (a share of the 5-hour limit, or dollars without subscription windows), from the person's own last three checks or a stated starting figure, and **Stop the check at** sets a limit that warns at 80% and asks Claude to stop at the limit. Filed tickets are watched while Claude Code is open: listed on the welcome with **Check again** for a closed one, a toast when one is closed or its problem is confirmed fixed, and the open ones in the band above the prompt.
