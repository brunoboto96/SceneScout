A guided check now says what went wrong in one plain sentence with one button, in place of an error message: the site's address does not open or the test browser could not be downloaded (Try again), the sign-in ran out part-way (Sign in again), or the check reached its time limit and stopped before writing up what it found (See all problems). The status pane and the guided panel show the screen, and the agent is given the same sentence to tell the person. The safety explainer ("Is it safe to run on my live site?") can now be opened from the care-level choice in the panel and goes back to the check being set up, and the Safety model page in the guide quotes it word for word.
