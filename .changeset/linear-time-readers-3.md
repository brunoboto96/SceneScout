The last readers of app text that took time quadratic in a crafted string now read it in linear time: a page address's fragment when a flow step's load fails, a box-shadow's lengths and colour in the design audit, and a chosen option, an attached file and a finding's title in the plain report. A long run of hash marks, full stops, `rgb(`, quotes or tabs took over a minute at 1 MB and now takes a fraction of a second. Results are unchanged.
