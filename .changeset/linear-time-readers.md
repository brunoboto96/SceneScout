Ticket text, routes a lane reports, request paths in a lane's evidence and the labels, URLs and bodies the write policy judges are read on the linear-time regular-expression engine, so a crafted string (a route of hyphenated words or a long run of slashes, a ticket heading edged by a run of colons) no longer stalls a run: each took minutes to hours at 1 MB and now takes under a second. Results are unchanged, except that a label such as `Sign-offſ` is now refused as a sign-off.
