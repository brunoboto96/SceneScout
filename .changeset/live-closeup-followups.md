The live view's close-up keeps its session's stream in step with the rest of the page: pressing "Stream all" while a close-up is open holds for that session after the close-up closes, and opening another session's close-up from the keyboard hands the first one back as it was; a card's own Stream toggle pressed behind the close-up is a choice that outlasts it. Its Stream button disappears with a session that closes under it, and a still that fails to capture once stays on screen instead of blanking the picture until the next refresh.
