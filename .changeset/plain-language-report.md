The report now opens in plain words: a short summary, then each problem with its impact (blocks users, annoying or cosmetic), numbered steps, what was expected, what happened and its picture, with the technical detail folded beneath each one. `report.md` puts this section before the technical report, and `report.html` opens on it. `scout_report {report}` chooses the parts: `both` (default), `qa` for the plain section alone, or `dev` for the technical report alone.
