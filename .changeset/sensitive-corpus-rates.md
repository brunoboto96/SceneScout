A field named as a passphrase in two words ("Pass phrase", `pass_phrase`, `passPhrase`) is now taken for a secret field, so what is typed into it is masked. The Safety model guide also publishes the measured miss and false-alarm rates of the redaction (secret fields, typed values, secret parameters, email addresses, phone numbers and card numbers), from a seeded generated corpus that a test holds equal to a fresh run.
