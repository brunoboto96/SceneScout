Release notes for the spring update

What changed in your workspace

Projects now open where you left them, and the list of recent files keeps its order between sessions on every device you use.

Sharing a project with a team now takes one step instead of three.

Faster search across every project

Search reads titles, descriptions and comments, and it narrows its results as you type rather than after you press enter.

Settings you can now change

The default view, the first day of the week and the time zone used in exported reports are now kept per workspace.

Notifications you receive

A weekly summary replaces the daily emails, and you can still turn the daily emails back on from your profile settings.

Accessibility improvements

Every dialog now returns focus to the control that opened it when it closes.
